# Requirements

Status: draft; not yet agreed.

## Business problem

A fictional consumer-goods manufacturer needs to trace finished products through components and packaging, identify broken structures and calculate material quantities. Packaging analysts and master-data stewards need an explainable result at a stated effective date.

This case demonstrates engineering for packaging analysis. It is not a certified regulatory calculation and makes no claim of real compliance savings.

## Proposed acceptance scenarios

A hand-calculated small BOM matches rolled-up quantities; shared components contribute through every valid path; cycles terminate and remain visible; reverse impact finds all affected roots; changing effective date selects the correct relationships; invalid units cannot silently enter material totals.

## To resolve at discovery

Define stakeholder decisions, included and excluded scope, data sources, refresh/latency expectations, environment constraints and a traceable acceptance checklist. Link each acceptance criterion to evidence when implemented.
